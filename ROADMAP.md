# Roadmap

Where Providence is headed. This is direction, not a schedule; plans change as
the beta gets used. The [CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md) covers what has shipped.

## Now: a sturdier beta

- Clear errors instead of dropped connections when a request is too large or
  takes too long, and the ability to cancel slow requests.
- Saves that stay correct after every kind of edit, checked against a full save.
- Safer handling of damaged or unusual scenario files and archives.

## Next: easier to start and to use

- Playtest from the spot you are editing.
- A welcome screen, a small sample project and a short tutorial.
- Plain-language step labels and diagnostics, with developer details behind a
  toggle, and one Apply action everywhere.
- Light, dark and high-contrast themes.
- An accessibility pass across the editor.

## Ideas and problems

Open an issue for a bug or an idea. Bug reports are most useful with the exact
version, scenario, tool and steps to reproduce.
